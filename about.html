About Us

Glow Sniff is a candle brand dedicated to creating warm, relaxing and memorable scents for every space. Our candles are carefully crafted to bring comfort, calmness, and a touch of elegance into your everyday life.

Inspired by nature and modern living, each Glow Sniff candle is made using high-quality wax and premium fragrances. We believe a candle should do more than just smell good — it should create an atmosphere that makes you feel at home.

Meet the Founder

Glow Sniff was founded by Ana Micaela C. Gelig, who dreamed of building her own business from a young age. Her passion for creativity and self-care inspired her to start a brand that focuses on relaxation and beautiful home experiences.

With a love for fragrances and cozy environments, Ana Micaela C. Gelig created Glow Sniff to share candles that help people unwind after a long day. Each product is designed with care, combining style, quality, and soothing scents.
